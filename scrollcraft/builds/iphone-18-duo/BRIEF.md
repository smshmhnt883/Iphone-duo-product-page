# Brief: iPhone 18 Duo

Creative Direction & Production Architecture: Senior Creative Technologist / Lead Motion Engineer

## 1. Vibe & Design Language
- **Words**: Precision, monolithic, aerodynamic, tactile, transcendent luxury.
- **Visual References**:
  - Apple keynote product film lighting (Greg Koenig industrial tear-down aesthetic).
  - Denis Villeneuve *Dune* ornithopter geometry meets Dieter Rams *Less but better*.
  - Bang & Olufsen luxury tactile audio surfaces and sapphire crystal watchmaking.

## 2. Page Grammar
- **Grammar**: **Filmic One-Shot meets Chaptered Live Surface** (Scroll Scrub Sequence with Pinned Dimensional Typography).
- **Core Mechanism**: 60fps/120fps canvas-driven frame scrubber synchronized to scroll velocity with inertia dampening and interactive pointer parallax.

## 3. Feeling Curve
- **Act 1 (The Reveal)**: *Awe & anticipation* — Emergence from deep studio shadows into razor-sharp titanium rim highlights.
- **Act 2 (The Unfold / The Duo Mechanism)**: *Wonder & mechanical satisfaction* — The dual chassis articulates from 0° folded to 180° seamless dual canvas.
- **Act 3 (Optical Architecture)**: *Sensory intimacy* — Extreme macro glide across sapphire camera prisms with antireflective violet lens flare.
- **Act 4 (Internal Alchemy / A20 Silicon)**: *Technical reverence* — Controlled micro-explosion of internal graphene vapor chamber, battery arrays, and neural silicon.
- **Act 5 (The Horizon / Culmination)**: *Desire & finality* — Full lockup, synchronized dual OLED ignition, definitive CTA.

## 4. The Peak
- **The Moment**: "The moment when the two halves open in zero gravity and the crease between them literally evaporates into one unbroken glass canvas."
- **Location**: Act 2 (The Unfold).

## 5. The Tell-Someone Sentence
- *"It's the site where you control the unboxing and physical opening of the dual-screen iPhone with your scroll wheel down to every millimeter of the hinge."*

## 6. Signature Move
- **Dynamic Haptic Angle Dial**: A subtle radial angle indicator (0° -> 90° -> 180°) floating at the viewport corner that tracks the exact folding posture of the phone with interactive drag-to-fold control.

## 7. Asset Plan
- **Primary Sequence**: 60-120 sequential frames or 5-8s smooth 30fps video clip generated via Google Flow.
- **Resolution**: 16:9 4K / 1440p master downsampled with FFmpeg into high-density WebP/MP4 scrub buffers.
